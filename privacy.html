Privacy Policy

chinese-videos · Last updated 21 November 2026

What this application is

chinese-videos is a private Meta app used by a single person, its operator (Bastien Ratat), to publish short videos to Facebook Pages that the operator owns and administers. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it has no users other than the operator. There is no sign-up: no one else can connect to it or grant it access to their account or their Pages.

What data it accesses

When the operator signs in with Facebook, the app receives, with the permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts:

  • the list of Facebook Pages the operator administers, with each Page's name, identifier and access token;
  • the ability to create posts on those Pages.

That is all. The app does not read, collect or store anything about the people who view, like, follow or comment on those Pages. It requests no profile information, no friend list, no email address, no contact list, no advertising data. It runs no analytics and sets no cookies on this page.

What it does with it

The Page identifiers and access tokens are stored on the operator's own computer and are used for one purpose: uploading and scheduling the operator's own videos on the operator's own Pages through Meta's Graph API. The videos, their captions and their publication dates are kept in a private database that only the operator can reach.

What it does not do

The data is never sold, rented, shared, published or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any automated decision-making. It is not combined with data from another source. No other person or company is given access to it.

How long it is kept

Access tokens are kept while the app is in use and are deleted when it stops being used, when a token is revoked, or on request. The videos and their captions are the operator's own work and are kept as part of the operator's records.

Withdrawing access, and deleting data

The operator can disconnect the app at any time from Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, then remove chinese-videos. Removing it immediately invalidates the app's access tokens and ends its ability to post.

To ask that any data held by this app be deleted, write to bastien.ratat@gmail.com. Because the app holds only Page identifiers and access tokens belonging to its own operator, honouring such a request means deleting those stored credentials; this is done within 30 days and confirmed by reply.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, a person whose personal data is processed has the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Any such request can be sent to bastien.ratat@gmail.com and will be answered within one month. Since this app processes no personal data about anyone other than its own operator, such a request is in practice limited to that operator's own credentials.

Changes

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
